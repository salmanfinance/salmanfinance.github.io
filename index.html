About Me

A professional journey built through practical accounting experience, continuous learning and exposure to different business environments.

I am Salmanul Faris, a Senior Accountant and Finance Professional with more than 5 years of practical experience in accounting, finance operations, reporting and business support across India and the UAE.

My professional journey started in Kerala, India, where I gained practical experience in accounting, online sales coordination and business operations within a manufacturing environment. This early experience helped me understand that accounting is not only about entering transactions, but also about understanding how a business actually operates.

After moving to the UAE, I expanded my experience in finance and accounting within manufacturing and other business environments. My responsibilities have included financial reporting, general ledger management, bank reconciliation, accounts payable, accounts receivable, supplier and customer follow-up, ageing analysis, cash flow monitoring, expense control and VAT-related support.

Currently, I work across Werner Generators LLC, Exigen LLC and Radiant Energy, supporting accounting and finance activities across manufacturing, trading and rental business operations.

I have developed practical exposure to accounting platforms and ERP tools including Tally Prime, Tally ERP, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Peachtree, SAP FICO and Microsoft Excel.

My objective through this website is to build a professional knowledge and career portfolio where I can document my experience, share useful accounting and finance articles, showcase certifications and achievements, and connect with professionals, businesses and future opportunities.
