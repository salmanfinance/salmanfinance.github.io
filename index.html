About Me

A practical accounting and finance professional with experience across multiple industries and business environments.

I am Salmanul Faris, a Senior Accountant and Finance Professional with 5+ years of experience in accounting, finance operations and reporting across manufacturing, chemicals, trading and multi-industry environments.

I started my professional journey in Kerala, India, where I gained practical experience in accounting, online sales coordination and business operations in a manufacturing environment.

After moving to the UAE, I expanded my experience in finance and accounting operations, including financial reporting, bank reconciliation, accounts payable, accounts receivable, supplier management, customer follow-up, cash flow monitoring and VAT-related accounting support.

My professional experience has given me exposure to different business models and operational environments. I have worked with manufacturing, chemical, trading and rental-related businesses and developed a practical understanding of how accounting connects with day-to-day business operations.

Currently, I work across Werner Generators LLC, Exigen LLC and Radiant Energy, supporting accounting and finance activities across manufacturing, trading and rental business operations.

My work involves maintaining accounting records, preparing reports, reconciliations, monitoring receivables and payables, reviewing expenses, supporting management information and helping maintain financial control.

I believe that a good accountant should understand more than accounting entries. Understanding business operations, cash flow, customers, suppliers, costs and internal processes is equally important for providing useful financial information.

Through Salman Finance, I want to document my professional journey, share practical accounting and finance knowledge, publish useful articles and build a professional platform that represents my skills, experience, certifications, projects and future career development.
