About Me

I am Salmanul Faris, a Senior Accountant and Finance Professional with 5+ years of experience in accounting, finance operations, and reporting across manufacturing, chemicals, trading, and multi-industry environments.


I started my professional journey in Kerala, India, where I gained practical experience in accounting, online sales coordination, and business operations in a manufacturing environment.


After moving to the UAE, I developed my expertise in finance and accounting operations, handling financial reporting, reconciliations, accounts payable, accounts receivable, VAT support, and supplier management.

My goal is to use my accounting knowledge, ERP skills, and business understanding to support organizations with accurate financial information and efficient finance processes.
