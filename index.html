About Me

I am Salmanul Faris, a Senior Accountant and Finance Professional with 5+ years of experience in accounting, finance operations and reporting across manufacturing, chemicals, trading and multi-industry environments.

I started my professional journey in Kerala, India, where I gained practical experience in accounting, online sales coordination and business operations within a manufacturing environment.

After moving to the UAE, I developed my expertise in finance and accounting operations, including financial reporting, reconciliations, accounts payable, accounts receivable, VAT support, supplier management and daily finance operations.

Currently, I work as Senior Accountant & Finance Executive, handling accounting and finance activities across Werner Generators LLC, Exigen LLC and Radiant Energy, supporting manufacturing, trading and rental business operations.

My experience across different industries has helped me develop a practical understanding of financial management, accounting systems, reporting and business operations.

My professional focus is to maintain accurate financial information, improve finance processes and support businesses with reliable accounting and reporting.
